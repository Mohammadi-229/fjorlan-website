Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting a project with us.

How fast is delivery?+

Most projects are delivered within a few days, depending on scope and complexity. Larger projects like full book illustrations may take longer — we'll always give you a clear timeline upfront.

Can I request revisions?+

Yes — revisions are part of every custom project. We want you fully happy with the final result before it's considered complete.

How does pricing work?+

Each service has a starting price listed on our Services page. Final pricing depends on project scope, and we'll confirm the exact cost with you before starting any work.

How do I start a project?+

Just fill out our project request form with your details and budget. We typically respond within 24 hours to discuss next steps.

Is the work actually AI-generated?+

We use AI-powered tools to work faster, but every project gets real creative direction, editing, and quality checks from our team before it reaches you — it's not raw, unedited AI output.

What payment methods do you accept?+

We'll share payment details directly with you once your project is confirmed, based on what's most convenient for your location.

Do you offer refunds?+

Since each project is custom work, refund eligibility depends on the stage of progress. Reach out to us directly if you have concerns — we'll always try to make it right.
